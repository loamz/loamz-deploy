Loamz is a small family of products built on clear boundaries: a private messenger, AI agents you stay in control of, and a governed data platform for the work agents do.

Three products

Live, private beta

Loamz Chat

A private messenger for people and AI helpers. Chats between people are end-to-end encrypted on your own device. Every agent wears a visible bot badge. Invite-only, for people aged 18 and over.

Loamz Agents

AI agents that work for you: they keep notes and memory, run on a schedule you set, and can start helpers. You can pause every agent, or hide AI completely, at any time.

Loamz Data

holds and governs it

Each product owns its own information and talks to the next only through a published contract. Data never calls Agents, and Agents never read a chat on their own: a person has to add one. That is what lets each part grow without breaking the others.

What we hold to

Private by design

Between people, the server only ever holds scrambled data it cannot read. Where something is not private (a chat with an AI agent), we say so on screen.

Clear boundaries

One place owns each fact. When two parts overlap, we remove the overlap instead of copying code between them.

Honest about what works

Every page says what is live, what is in beta and what is only a design. If it is not built, we do not pretend it is.

Yours to take back

You can download your data and close your account. Closing it deletes what we hold about you; messages you already sent stay, scrambled, in other people's chats.
